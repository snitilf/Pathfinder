## Verification (optional)

Use independent verification for substantial or risky changes and whenever the user requests it. Small changes need relevant checks, not an automatic extra agent.

Give `pathfinder:verifier` the claimed outcome and the changed paths or diff. Ask for CONFIRMED, REFUTED, or INCOMPLETE with evidence. Security-sensitive and financial changes need checks of abuse cases and trust boundaries. The verifier reports findings without fixing them; make corrections in the main session and recheck affected conditions.

Built-in `/code-review` and `/verify` are also available for their documented purposes. A passing review supports only the conditions actually checked. Report skipped checks and unresolved risks before claiming completion.
